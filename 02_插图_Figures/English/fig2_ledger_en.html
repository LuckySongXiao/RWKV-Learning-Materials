WKV account book: for each new word, only two steps
RWKV-4 Time-Mixing's recurrent skeleton — the secret to constant memory
1

Read accounts (output the answer for this step)

wkvₜ = (aₜ₋₁ + e^(u+kₜ)·vₜ) ÷ (bₜ₋₁ + e^(u+kₜ))
Old accounts a, b
The two account books left from the previous step (fixed size)
＋
New token vote e^(u+k)·v
Current token content v multiplied by speaking weight
→
Weighted average = output
New numerator ÷ new denominator: one division completes 'account check'
↓ After accounting, pass to next step
2

Bookkeeping (update two accounts)

aₜ = e^(−w)·aₜ₋₁ + e^(kₜ)·vₜ　　bₜ = e^(−w)·bₜ₋₁ + e^(kₜ)
Old account discounted e^(−w)
Each token, old content uniformly multiplied by a factor < 1 (forgetfulness adjustable)
＋
New content added e^(k)·v
Current token information written into account
→
New accounts aₜ, bₜ
Size unchanged! Whether reading 100 or 100,000 tokens
Manual example (discount 0.5, v = 2, 4, 8)

Step 1: Account = 2 ÷ 1 → Output2
Step 2: Account = (0.5×2+4) ÷ (0.5×1+1) = 5 ÷ 1.5 → Output3.33
Step 3: Account = (0.5×5+8) ÷ (0.5×1.5+1) = 10.5 ÷ 1.75 → Output6
Old content halved, new token speaks louder—this is 'remembering new things, forgetting old things'.

Why constant memory?

Regardless of sequence length, model only holdstwo accounts a and b(each fixed size d). Transformer keeps all T notes (memory ∝ T), RWKV only keeps 2 accounts (memory ∝ 1). This is what O(d) means in the table.
